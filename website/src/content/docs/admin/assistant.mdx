The **Assistant** is a chat in OADM. Ask it about your devices in your own words: which cameras are offline, which run
old AXIS OS, what a camera sees. It works with your rights and your devices only. A change is always a plan first: you
see what it would do and press **Run** yourself.

<Image src={panel} alt="The Assistant panel with an answer: a table of offline devices and a camera picture" class="screen" />

## Set it up

An administrator sets it up once for the server.

<Steps>

1. Open the page **MCP & Assistant** in the rail, the card **Assistant**.
2. Choose the **Provider**: Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama or OpenAI-compatible (LM Studio, vLLM, Azure OpenAI).
3. Enter the **API key**, or the **Address** for Ollama and OpenAI-compatible (`http://10.0.0.5:11434`).
4. Set the **Daily limit** in tokens for the whole server and, if you want, per user.
5. Click **Test**, then **Turn on**.

</Steps>

<Image src={settings} alt="The page MCP & Assistant, card Assistant: provider, API key, pictures, daily limits" class="screen" />

With Anthropic and OpenAI, device names, addresses and other data of your devices go to that provider. Ollama keeps
everything in your network. Camera pictures go to the model only with **Send pictures to the model** on.

The API key is stored encrypted on the server and never shown again. Who may chat: the permission **Use the assistant**
on the [Permissions](../users/) page. Administrators have it.

## Chat

Click the chat button in the title bar. The panel stays open while you move between pages.

- **Model**: choose it next to the send arrow. OADM remembers your choice on this computer.
- **Selected devices**: the devices you select on the Devices page go with your message. Ask "restart these cameras" and
  the assistant works on them.
- **Attach file**: for a firmware or application file. You can also drop files on the panel or paste them into the
  message box. A pasted picture is attached as a PNG file. Files go to the OADM server, never to the provider.
- **Stop** or Escape ends an answer. **Clear** empties the chat and starts over.
- **Try again** under the last answer asks the same again. **Edit** next to your last message, or Up arrow in the empty
  box, lets you change it and send it again.
- Scrolled up, the list stays where you are. **Go to the latest** takes you back to the end.
- Type @ or click **Choose devices** to pick devices for your question. They go with the message as one chip.
- Ctrl+J opens the assistant. **Copy chat** copies the whole conversation.

An empty panel shows your device counts and suggestions for the page you are on; click one to ask it. Device addresses
in an answer are links: click to select the device, or right-click to open its live view or web interface. Tables have
**Copy as CSV** and **Save as CSV**.

Tables in an answer are devices: click a row to select it on the Devices page, or **Select all in the grid**.

After **Run**, the plan shows what happened. When some devices failed, **Try the failed devices again** prepares the
same change for them; press **Run** again.

## Changes need your click

The assistant never changes a device by itself. It asks OADM for a plan and shows it as a card: what Run does, taken from
the plan itself (a rule's event and where it sends, the tag names, the file), on how many devices, which devices are
skipped and why, and the warnings. **Run** starts the tasks; the card follows them. A **Run** that deletes is red.
**Cancel** drops the plan.

<Image src={preview} alt="Two plan cards: save a rule with its event and target, and delete two tags with a red Run" class="screen" />

Every change and every camera picture is in the audit log under your name, as if you did it in OADM.

## What it cannot do

- Read or set passwords, keys or tokens.
- Change users, roles, device groups, the license, plugins or server settings.
- Show live video.
- Remember a chat: closing the panel or signing out forgets it.

It can answer questions about OADM itself from this documentation.

## Limits

When the daily limit is reached, the assistant says so and works again the next day. One answer runs at a time, with at
most 20 steps. An answer that takes longer than 2 minutes stops with "The provider did not answer. Try again."

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No chat button in the title bar | The assistant is off, or your role lacks **Use the assistant**. |
| "This model cannot use tools. Choose another one." | Choose another model next to **Send**. Many small Ollama models cannot. |
| "The assistant reached today's limit. It works again tomorrow." | Wait, or raise the **Daily limit** in Settings. |
| **Test** shows an error from the provider | Check the API key or the address and that the server reaches the provider. |
